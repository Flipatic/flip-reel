# Support

- **Questions and integration help:** [GitHub Discussions, Q&A](https://github.com/Flipatic/flip-reel/discussions/categories/q-a).
  Include the FlipReel version, your framework or CMS, and a minimal feed if relevant.
- **Bugs:** [open an issue](https://github.com/Flipatic/flip-reel/issues/new/choose) using the
  bug form.
- **Feature ideas:** [Discussions, Ideas](https://github.com/Flipatic/flip-reel/discussions/categories/ideas).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Documentation:** [API reference](docs/reference) and [architecture](docs/architecture.md).

FlipReel is maintained on a best-effort basis. There is no guaranteed response time for
community support.

Publishers who want help rolling FlipReel out on their sites, or want to produce the videos
with [Flipatic](https://flipatic.com), can write to Michał Samojlik at
[michal@flipatic.com](mailto:michal@flipatic.com).
